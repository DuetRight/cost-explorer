Direct swap

Acrylic or solid-surface

Range
$7,000–$12,000
Typically 3–5 working days
  • Tub out, one-piece or multi-piece shower in — same footprint, same drain
  • New valve trim, glass door or curtain rod, patch and paint
  • Permanent, code-compliant, genuinely easy to clean
Anyone promising one day is selling a liner over your old tub — different product, different lifespan.

No price bands. On purpose.

We haven't published kitchen price bands yet — deliberately. Kitchen costs swing harder than any other room: cabinets alone can run from stock to custom-millwork money, and owner-supplied appliances change everything. A flat range we couldn't stand behind would be marketing, not information.

What we do instead: a scoped, line-item estimate — the same detailed format our bathroom clients get, where every number is attached to a decision you control.
